If you've asked three studios in Dubai to quote the same five-page website, you probably got three numbers that aren't even in the same ballpark. That's not you misreading the quotes. It's how this market works, and it's exactly why "what's a fair startup website cost in Dubai?" is so hard to answer straight.

So I did the boring part for you. I pulled six Dubai website pricing guides published or updated in 2026, lined up their numbers side by side, and mapped what an early-stage startup should actually spend at each stage. Here's the honest version.

## Startup website cost in Dubai: the short answer

Most early-stage startups in Dubai should budget AED 3,000 to 8,000 for a single landing page and AED 8,000 to 25,000 for a 5 to 15 page marketing site from a small studio, based on 2026 agency price guides. Running costs add roughly AED 800 or more a year. Arabic, VAT, and maintenance come on top.

## Why the same website gets wildly different quotes

When two quotes for "a 6-page website" are AED 15,000 apart, the difference almost always comes from these four things.

### Template or custom design

A AED 1,200 site is nearly always a theme with your logo and colors swapped in. A AED 15,000 site should include custom design: layouts built around your offer and your buyer, not someone else's. Neither is wrong. Just know which one you're buying.

### Who writes the words

Copy is the line item most often missing from cheaper quotes. If the agency says "you'll send us the content," congrats, you're now the copywriter. For a startup, the words on your homepage do more selling than any animation, so ask who owns them.

### The platform

Webflow, WordPress, Framer, Shopify, or fully custom code all mean different build effort and different monthly fees. For most startup marketing sites, I'd pick a no-code platform your team can edit without calling a developer every time you change a headline.

### What "done" includes

Revision rounds, mobile testing, basic on-page SEO, analytics setup, a handover session, and who owns the files at the end. Two quotes for the same page count can differ by half of that list.

## What to spend at each startup stage

The smartest way to set a website budget is to tie it to your stage, not your ambition. Your positioning will change a lot between your first 10 customers and your first 100, and your website should be able to change with it.

![Three-stage website budget for startups in Dubai: validate, launch, and scale, with AED ranges and timelines](/journal/startup-website-budget-by-stage.webp "startup-website-budget-by-stage")

### Stage 1: Validating (AED 3,000 to 8,000)

You're pre-product or pre-revenue and testing whether anyone cares. You need one landing page with a sharp headline, a short explanation, some proof, and one clear action: join the waitlist, book a demo, or message you on WhatsApp. Expect 1 to 2 weeks.

Spending much more here usually means polishing a message that will change in three months.

### Stage 2: Launching (AED 8,000 to 25,000)

Your product is live and you're chasing your first real customers, or getting ready to raise. Now you need 5 to 15 pages: home, product or features, pricing, about, contact, and maybe a blog. Expect 3 to 6 weeks.

This is where custom design starts to pay for itself, because investors and early customers judge your credibility in about the time it takes to scroll your homepage.

### Stage 3: Scaling (AED 25,000 to 70,000+)

You've raised, you have a sales or marketing team, and the website is now a lead engine. That means a proper CMS, CRM and analytics integrations, and a system for spinning up campaign landing pages fast. Expect 6 weeks or more. Digital Nexa's 2026 guide puts corporate sites at AED 25,000 to 60,000 and lead generation sites at AED 30,000 to 70,000.

My honest take: if you're pre-seed and someone quotes you AED 40,000, you're probably paying for Stage 3 polish with Stage 1 information. ### Don't forget VAT

UAE VAT is 5%, and a business has to register once its taxable supplies pass AED 375,000 a year, according to the [UAE government's official VAT guidance](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/finance-and-investment/taxation/vat/valueaddedtaxvat). Most established agencies will add 5% to your invoice. A solo freelancer below the threshold may not.

So before you compare two quotes, ask one simple question: is this plus VAT? On a AED 20,000 build, that's AED 1,000 you didn't plan for.

## What adding Arabic really costs

Since none of the six guides put a number on it, here's how to estimate it yourself.

- **Translation.** Published agency rates run about AED 0.30 to 0.40 per word (Lingo, 2024) or AED 150 to 300 per page (Kolonell, 2026). A 10-page startup site with around 4,000 words of copy lands somewhere around AED 1,500 to 3,000.
- **Right-to-left (RTL) layout.** Arabic reads right to left, so menus, icons, forms, and sliders need to be mirrored, not just right-aligned. Kolonell estimates RTL adds 20 to 35% to front-end work.
- **Arabic typography.** Free typefaces like IBM Plex Sans Arabic or Noto Kufi Arabic are perfectly solid for a startup. Licensed Arabic web fonts can run AED 2,000 to 10,000, per Kolonell.
- **QA time.** Every page now exists twice. Somebody has to check both versions on mobile.

Do you need Arabic on day one? If you sell to UAE consumers, government, or semi-government buyers, plan it from the very first wireframe, even if the Arabic version goes live a few weeks after English. If you're an English-first B2B SaaS, launch in English and add Arabic in Stage 2.

Either way, design for RTL from the start. Then adding Arabic later is a translation job, not a rebuild. And please skip machine translation for anything a customer will read.

## FAQ

### How much does a basic website cost in Dubai?

Across six 2026 price guides from Dubai agencies, a basic business website ranges from about AED 1,200 to AED 30,000. The low end is usually a template with your branding. From around AED 8,000, you can typically expect custom design from a small studio.

### Is a AED 1,000 website worth it?

It can be, if you need something live this week and treat it as a placeholder. Just know you're buying a template, and you'll likely rebuild it once you have real customers and a clearer message. For a launch site that has to win trust, it's rarely enough.

### How long does it take to build a startup website in Dubai?

A landing page usually takes 1 to 2 weeks, and a 5 to 15 page site takes about 3 to 6 weeks, based on 2026 Dubai agency guides. The real bottleneck is almost always content: copy, photos, and getting sign-off from co-founders.

### Do web designers in Dubai charge VAT?

If they're VAT-registered, yes, at 5%. Registration is mandatory once a business passes AED 375,000 in taxable supplies a year, so most agencies charge it and some small freelancers don't. Always ask whether a quote includes VAT before you compare it.

### Do I need an Arabic version of my startup website?

It depends on who buys from you. Consumer brands and anyone selling to government or semi-government buyers should plan for Arabic early. English-first B2B startups can launch in English and add Arabic later, as long as the design supports RTL from the start.
